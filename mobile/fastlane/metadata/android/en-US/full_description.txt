SharedMoney helps friends, roommates, families, and travel groups keep shared expenses clear.

Create a group, add expenses, choose who paid, and split costs across the people involved. SharedMoney keeps a shared ledger of what happened and shows simple balances so everyone can see who owes who.

Use SharedMoney for:

- Trips and vacations
- Roommate rent, utilities, and household costs
- Dinner groups and shared plans
- Family expenses
- Any group that needs a clear record of shared spending

SharedMoney focuses on tracking and settlement records. Payments happen outside the app in the payment method your group already prefers.

Key features:

- Create groups for different plans or households
- Add shared expenses with clear participants
- Track balances across group members
- Record settlements when someone pays back
- Invite people to groups
- Keep every expense, split, and settlement in one shared record

No more guessing, screenshots, or messy notes. SharedMoney gives your group a simple answer to the question: who owes who?
